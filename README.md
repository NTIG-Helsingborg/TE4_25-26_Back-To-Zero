# Back To Zero

## ⚠️ Important: Firewall Warning

**This project does not have code signing**, which means Windows Firewall may block the application.

To run this project, you must either:

1. **Run as Administrator** - Right-click the executable and select "Run as administrator"
2. **Allow through Firewall** - Right-click the launch file → Properties → Allow app through firewall

---

A dark, Berserk-inspired 2D roguelike where your own blood is your greatest weapon. Your HP is your mana — cast spells, empower attacks, and harvest life-essence to survive as you confront undead beings in a dying world.

## Gameplay

**Blood Magic System**: Every ability costs health to cast. Manage your life force carefully — your HP bar is both your survival and your power source.

**Harvest Mechanic**: When enemies fall below 20% health, harvest their life-essence to restore your own health and charge your ultimate ability.

**Combat Abilities**: Wield blood-forged weapons and spells including Blood Knife, Blood Spear, Blood Slash, Blood Explosion, Blood Shield, Blood Bind, and more.

**Roguelike Progression**: Discover artifacts, collect items, and build your power as you fight through procedurally challenging encounters.

## Getting Started

Run `Back To Zero.exe` to launch the game.
